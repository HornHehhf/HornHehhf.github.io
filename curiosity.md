# Curiosity

I enjoy exploring questions simply because I am curious about them, and I love seeing and supporting others who want to do the same.
If there is something you genuinely want to understand, you have already spent some time exploring it, and **you think I might be helpful**, I would be happy to hear about it.
At the moment, I can be most helpful with **AI or AI+X**.
This is informal, curiosity-driven exploration—not a research program, internship, or formal mentorship. There is no expected outcome; the point is simply to explore something because we genuinely want to understand it.

## Share an Exploration

If you have something you would like to explore with me, **[share your exploration here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfR-19kZEwCLCkvXmMaN4_HJZ10UH87lL9rAdPj49Gv_mUebw/viewform?usp=publish-editor)**.
I may not be able to respond to every submission. I will generally respond when I think I can be helpful and have the capacity to do so. There is no guarantee of a response, meeting, or any particular amount or duration of support.
Please only share things you are comfortable discussing openly. Do not share confidential or sensitive information.

## Still Looking for Your Curiosity?

If you haven't yet found something you're deeply curious about, try exploring with AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT). Follow whatever catches your attention and see whether it leads to something you would keep exploring simply out of curiosity.
